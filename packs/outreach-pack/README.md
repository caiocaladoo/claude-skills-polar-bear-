# Claude Guide for Outreach: 32 Claude Skills

32 Claude skills for opening conversations with people you do not yet know, where Claude does the research and you write and send every message. For founders, independents and sellers.

**Guide and download:** [90n.ai/resources/outreach-pack](https://90n.ai/resources/outreach-pack)

## What this is

One outreach run, in order. You decide who to reach and check for a warm path before anything cold. Claude reads the public pages you point it to and lists what it found about the company and the person, with a link and a date on every fact and the unknowns written as unknown. You find a true reason to write, write the message yourself from two or three points, have it checked, and send about ten at a time. Then you read the replies, prepare the first call, write the recap and look at what your own numbers say. Each skill is one artifact, and each one hands over to the next.

The red line: Claude researches and prepares; you choose the people, write every word and press send: nothing is scraped, automated or sent for you, and a fact about someone with no source is dropped, never softened.

```
Decide who to reach -> Research the company -> Research the person -> Find the reason to write -> Write it yourself -> Check and send in small runs -> From reply to meeting -> Review what worked
```

## The skills

### 1 · Decide who to reach

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Ideal Client Profile](skills/reach-ideal-client-profile/SKILL.md) | Situations and triggers, the job you are hired for, roles in the decision, red flags, a test on a past engagement | You say yes to anyone and cannot say who a first message should go to |
| [Buying Situations Map](skills/reach-buying-situations-map/SKILL.md) | Moments that start a need, seen or assumed, the outside sign for each, the ones you leave alone | You know who you are for, not what would make them need you this month |
| [Target Account List](skills/reach-target-account-list/SKILL.md) | Two-line brief, criteria checked yes, no or unknown with sources, the number you can work, drops with reasons | Your list is forty logos and no reason to write to any of them |
| [Warm Path Check](skills/reach-warm-path-check/SKILL.md) | Who you already know per account from your own data, closeness in your words, a verdict per account | You are about to write cold to a company where a former colleague works |

### 2 · Research the company

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Company Fact List](skills/reach-company-fact-list/SKILL.md) | Up to twelve numbered facts with date and link, claims dropped for no source, unknowns | You have twenty minutes and refuse to write from a guess |
| [Source Check](skills/reach-source-check/SKILL.md) | Publisher, original or copy, date and wording checked per source, facts to keep, fix or drop | A fact sounds right and you cannot say where it came from |
| [Company Research Brief](skills/reach-company-research-brief/SKILL.md) | Half a page citing only numbered facts, hypotheses as questions, why now or "no reason yet" | You have the facts and want to walk in with questions, not a pitch |

### 3 · Research the person

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Contact Shortlist](skills/reach-contact-shortlist/SKILL.md) | Roles that live with the problem, people publicly named in them, the one or two you choose, the warm path | You know the company and not who there would care |
| [Identity Check](skills/reach-identity-check/SKILL.md) | Each source tested for same name and same organisation, namesakes set aside, confirmed or not | The search returns three people with the same name |
| [Person Research Brief](skills/reach-person-research-brief/SKILL.md) | Sourced, dated facts about the person's work, what you share, unknowns, open questions | You want to write something specific and refuse to guess or pad |
| [Research Coverage Check](skills/reach-research-coverage-check/SKILL.md) | Research labelled enough, thin or nothing found, what a thin result lets you say, your call | The brief is three lines long and you are tempted to fill it |

### 4 · Find the reason to write

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Trigger Event Scan](skills/reach-trigger-event-scan/SKILL.md) | Recent public events with date and link, too old or too private set aside, both readings, pitch-hook test | You want a real reason to write now, not "just reaching out" |
| [Buying Signals Watchlist](skills/reach-buying-signals-watchlist/SKILL.md) | One or two public signs per account, the page to watch, how often, what you do when one appears | The account fits and nothing has happened there yet |
| [Reason to Write](skills/reach-reason-to-write/SKILL.md) | Reasons that serve the other person, the fact each rests on, pitch-in-disguise test, one left for you to choose | Every opening you think of turns into "let me tell you what I do" |
| [Useful Material Match](skills/reach-useful-material-match/SKILL.md) | Your own materials checked against this person's facts: none, one or two that fit, and what not to send | You want to share something useful and everything feels like a brochure |

### 5 · Write it yourself

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Writing Voice Guide](skills/reach-voice-guide/SKILL.md) | How you open, ask and sign off, words you use and never use, an AI-tell checklist, a before and after | Everything a tool drafts for you sounds like everyone else's |
| [Talking Points](skills/reach-talking-points/SKILL.md) | Two or three sourced points for the one reason you chose, no greeting, no assembled message | You want help thinking about what to say, not a machine-written message |
| [Cold Email Outline](skills/reach-cold-email-outline/SKILL.md) | Warm path and past thread check, a frame with your words in each slot, flags per line, one follow-up date | You must write to someone who does not know you and refuse to send spam |
| [Connection Request Note](skills/reach-connection-request/SKILL.md) | The true context per person, one specific detail, why connecting helps them, who comes off the list | A blank request will be ignored and a false "great to connect" is worse |
| [Cold Call Outline](skills/reach-cold-call-outline/SKILL.md) | Keywords for the opening, the reason, two questions, the ask, two common refusals, a short voicemail | You avoid the phone because you do not know what to say after hello |

### 6 · Check and send in small runs

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Message Checklist](skills/reach-message-checklist/SKILL.md) | Six checks in order, a pass or a flag per check with the line, no replacement wording | You wrote it yourself and cannot tell whether it reads as one more pitch |
| [Grammar Check](skills/reach-grammar-check/SKILL.md) | Grammar, spelling and punctuation only, every change shown, risky fixes asked as questions | You want it clean without it stopping sounding like you |
| [Outreach Rules Check](skills/reach-outreach-rules-check/SKILL.md) | Channel, country and address type, the questions official pages raise, unknowns, questions for an adviser | You write across borders and do not know which rule applies |
| [Run of Ten Plan](skills/reach-run-of-ten/SKILL.md) | About ten people this week, the time block, one reason and one status each, the next run's start | Prospecting always comes last, then forty rushed notes go out |
| [Follow-Up Tracker](skills/reach-follow-up-tracker/SKILL.md) | Replies you owe first, one follow-up per silent thread, where each thread closes, calls to book | Good conversations die because nobody wrote back |

### 7 · From reply to meeting

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Reply Triage](skills/reach-reply-triage/SKILL.md) | What the reply says and asks, facts it adds, points for your answer, "stop" recorded at once | A reply arrives and you cannot tell interest from politeness |
| [Conversation Opener](skills/reach-conversation-opener/SKILL.md) | One reason, points from the real exchange, an ask sized to how little you know each other, an easy no | They accepted the connection and the next message is where most people pitch |
| [First Call Prep](skills/reach-first-call-prep/SKILL.md) | One page: why they agreed, three open questions, what to listen for, what not to pitch, a short agenda | They said yes to a call and you fear turning it into a pitch |
| [Call Recap](skills/reach-call-recap/SKILL.md) | What they said, what each side promised and by when, the next step, points for your recap note | A week later neither of you remembers what was agreed |

### 8 · Review what worked

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Outreach Log](skills/reach-outreach-log/SKILL.md) | One private row per person: date, channel, reason, warm path, reply, call, open promises, do not contact | You cannot say who you wrote to last month or what you promised |
| [Outreach Review](skills/reach-outreach-review/SKILL.md) | Your own counts by reason, channel and warm or cold, expected against actual, one thing to change | You have no way to tell what worked |
| [Next Run Test](skills/reach-next-run-test/SKILL.md) | One change, what you expect, what you count, how many runs before you look, keep or drop | You keep changing five things at once and learn nothing |

## Example prompts

- "Run reach-target-account-list. My brief: I help operations leaders after a failed system rollout. Here are fifteen organisations I have in mind."
- "Run reach-company-fact-list on this organisation. Read only these three public pages and give me numbered facts with a date and a link. Drop anything with no source."
- "Run reach-warm-path-check with my connections export and this list of eight accounts. Tell me who I already know and let me correct how well I know them."
- "Run reach-talking-points. The reason I chose is to share my checklist. Give me two or three points with sources. Do not write the message."
- "Run reach-message-checklist on the email below. Flags only, no replacement wording."
- "Run reach-outreach-review on my log for the last six runs. Use my counts only and tell me what the numbers cannot show."

## Quality bar

- Every fact about a company or a person carries a link and a date, or it is marked as your own first-hand history. A fact with no source is dropped.
- Unknowns are written as unknown. A thin result is labelled thin and stays thin.
- Warm before cold: the warm path is checked before any cold message, and a run holds about ten people.
- Claude gives a reason, points, a frame and flags. It never drafts the message, builds a sequence, places a call or sends anything. Where a browser or mail connector is used, it reads and does nothing else, and it is never used on LinkedIn.
- Nothing grades, ranks, scores or profiles a person. Fit is checked for an organisation against your own brief, with yes, no or unknown.
- No invented numbers, reply rates or benchmarks. Reviews use your own counts.
- Points about consent, anti-spam and data protection are questions taken from official pages: check with a qualified adviser.
- The red line: Claude researches and prepares; you choose the people, write every word and press send: nothing is scraped, automated or sent for you, and a fact about someone with no source is dropped, never softened.
