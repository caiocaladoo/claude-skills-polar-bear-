# Consulting Proposal

## When To Use
The thinking is done and the options are priced. What remains is a document your contact will forward to people who never heard you speak. They will give it five minutes. Run this to write a proposal that puts your advice and the decision you are asking for at the top, on a foundation of their problem in their own words.

## When Not To Use
If the signer will read a single page, write the Proposal Executive Summary. If the client has already said yes and needs detail to sign against, use Statement of Work. The proposal persuades; the statement of work defines.

## Inputs
- Problem statement, call record with verbatim lines, and the playback the client confirmed
- Your three priced options and the scope with its won't list
- The proof you hold (case paragraphs with permission status), your bio as you write it, your AI use statement
If you have none of this, I start from your call notes and the option you favour, leave each gap as a [placeholder] and mark the proposal as a first draft.

## Approach
The structure is Barbara Minto's pyramid with situation, complication and question (barbaraminto.com): the answer leads, and every section supports the one above it. Leading with the answer can sound presumptuous before trust exists, so it rests on their problem told in their words, never on your credentials. The failure it prevents is three pages about your practice before the client meets their own problem on page four, followed by silence.

## Workflow
1. Ask, in one go: who will read this besides your contact? Which option do you recommend, and why? How long may it be (default: a five-minute read)?
2. Start at the top: the option you recommend, the outcome it buys and the dated decision you are asking for. If those three lines will not come, the proposal is not ready, and I will name what is missing.
3. Write situation, complication and question in the client's words. Situation is what they already accept. Complication is what changed or what is at stake, in their figures. The question is what they must decide. Quotation marks only around lines from the verbatim bin.
4. Then outcomes in their measures, how you would work (phases, and what they hold at the end of each) and the three options side by side with your prices and what each leaves out.
5. Proof and your background come after their problem, never ahead of it, and only from your files. Then your AI use statement, unchanged in substance.
6. Run the pyramid check line by line: does this sentence support the one above it, and can it be traced to your notes or files? A line that does neither is cut or marked [source needed]. Draft in Claude Docs if you use it, or as a document you export; you read it, edit it and send it.

## Output Format
```markdown
# Proposal
For: [client role and organisation] · From: [you] · Date: [date]
## Recommendation
[Option]: [the outcome it buys]. Decision asked of you: [what], by [date].
## Your situation and what rides on it
- Situation: [what they accept, their words] · Source: [line]
- Complication: [what changed, their figures only] · Source: [line]
- The question in front of you: [what they must decide]
## What you will have, and how the work runs
| Phase | What happens | What you hold at the end |
|---|---|---|
| [phase] | [activity] | [result in their measure] |
## Three ways to proceed
| | [Option A] | [Option B] | [Option C] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outcome and scope | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |
| Leaves out | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |
| Price | [your price] | [your price] | [your price] |
## Proof, who you work with, and how AI is used
[Case paragraph and its permission status] · [Your background, as you supplied it] · [Your AI use statement]
## Decision
[Client decider role] chooses an option by [date]; [you] send this proposal and offer [walk-through date] to go through it together.
```

## Done When
- The opening lines hold the recommendation and the dated decision asked
- Their problem appears before any sentence about you
- Each figure, quote and result can be traced to your notes or files; the rest are [placeholders]

## Quality Bar
- Words are marked as theirs only if they come from your notes or a consented transcript
- Prices are yours; options differ by outcome and none is a decoy
- AI use is stated plainly, never tucked away or minimised
- A section with nothing true to say is removed, not filled
- Facts come only from your notes and files; I invent no reference, result, number or quote, and you make the recommendation and send it yourself.
