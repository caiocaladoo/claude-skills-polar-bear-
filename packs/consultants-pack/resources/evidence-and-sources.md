# Evidence and sources

Research checked 11 October 2026.

## What this set claims

It claims that the 39 skills follow established, publicly documented methods for qualifying an opportunity, running discovery, structuring a problem, comparing options, pricing, proposing, presenting and reviewing, and that they are arranged in the order of one consulting engagement, from the client's brief to the signed scope.

## Deliberate design choices

These are 90n's own choices and are not findings of any source above.

- **The red line.** Claude structures, drafts and checks; it never invents a reference, a result, a number or a client quote, and you run the conversation, make the recommendation and send everything yourself. The sources describe methods; the division of work between Claude and the consultant is ours.
- **A team of desks, not a cast of characters.** The eight groups are presented as desks behind one consultant. No skill has a name, a persona or a voice of its own.
- **Honest about AI.** Every skill that touches the subject says what AI does well first, treats "do it in house with your own AI" as a real option, and states the consultant's own use of AI plainly. Nothing sells against AI.
- **No scoring of people.** Several methods are often used to rate individuals (champions, stakeholders, interviewees). Here they record roles and stated positions only. Options are compared without a weighted score unless the consultant asks for one and supplies the weights.
- **The consultant's numbers only.** No fee, rate, benchmark, effort share or result is supplied by a skill. Where a source suggests a figure, it is left out and the consultant sets it.
- **Adviser referrals.** Contract, tax, data protection and call recording points are flagged "check with a qualified adviser". Recordings and transcripts are used only with everyone's consent.
- **Roles only.** The set names the reader's role and nothing about the size, sector or type of anyone's organisation.
- **Order.** The thinking desks (Problem, Strategy) come before fees and the proposal, so the proposal recommends from real options. A consultant who prefers to do that thinking after signature can run those desks later; each skill stands alone.
