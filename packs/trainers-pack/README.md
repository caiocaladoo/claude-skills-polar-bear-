# Claude for Trainers and Facilitators: 32 Claude Skills

32 Claude skills for one session or programme, from the first brief to the follow-up weeks later. For corporate trainers, facilitators and independent consultants who run workshops.

**Guide and download:** [90n.ai/resources/trainers-pack](https://90n.ai/resources/trainers-pack)

## What this is

The skills follow a session in the order you live it. You frame it (the purpose brief, the call with the sponsor, the questions for participants), design it (the session plan, the workshop agenda, the time budget, the activities, the role plays, the virtual version), prepare the room (the invitation, the run sheet, the guide for a co-trainer, the slide outline, the handout), get ready to run it (openers, agreements and consent, a plan for the difficult moments, debrief questions, the capture from the wall), make it stick (the action plan, the practice task, the follow-up messages, spaced recall, and two skills for participants' managers), review it (the feedback form, the summary, the retro, the sponsor debrief) and take it beyond one session (the proposal, the programme, the handover to other trainers). Each skill produces one artifact from one method, says what to paste, where the method breaks, gives a fixed output template, a finish line and the next skill to run. Where a skill fits a Claude surface (Claude Docs and Claude Slides, both in beta, or a photo uploaded to the chat), it says so.

Every skill holds one line: Claude prepares the session and the follow-up; you read the room and decide what happens in it, no participant is ever graded, ranked or profiled, and nothing is recorded without their consent.

```
Frame it -> Design it -> Prepare the room -> Run it -> Make it stick -> Review and improve -> Beyond one session
```

## The skills

### 1 · Frame it

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Session Purpose Brief](skills/fac-session-purpose-brief/SKILL.md) | One-page brief, decide or inform, who must be in the room, the "should this be a session at all" verdict | "The client wants a workshop" and nobody has said what must be different afterwards |
| [Sponsor Kickoff Questions](skills/fac-sponsor-kickoff-questions/SKILL.md) | Question guide for the sponsor call, success at work in their words, who does what before and after, one-page call note | The sponsor books the room, disappears, then asks months later why nothing changed |
| [Pre-Session Survey](skills/fac-pre-session-survey/SKILL.md) | Five to seven questions for participants, the invitation line, an aggregate summary for the design | You are designing for a group you have never met and all you have is a job title |

### 2 · Design it

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Training Session Plan](skills/fac-training-session-plan/SKILL.md) | Timed flow for a session that teaches a skill, the practice share, the scripted first five minutes, the energy logic | The draft is a list of topics with a slide count, and practice is "if we have time" |
| [Workshop Agenda](skills/fac-workshop-agenda/SKILL.md) | Blocks marked diverge or converge, the exercise for each, group splits, a participant version and a facilitator version | The agenda is a list of topics and the group is supposed to leave with a decision |
| [Session Time Budget](skills/fac-session-time-budget/SKILL.md) | Minutes per outcome, real activity time, what stays, what moves, a cut list with reasons | The sponsor's deck is far too long for the slot and everything "must go in" |
| [Group Activity Bank](skills/fac-group-activity-bank/SKILL.md) | Two or three group methods per block as run lists, with source, virtual variant and fallback | Every session ends up with the same two breakout formats, and the group can tell |
| [Role-Play Practice Kit](skills/fac-role-play-practice-kit/SKILL.md) | Learning points, a good and a poor model, role cards for trios, three ways to take part, observer notes, debrief | Role play is the part everyone dreads, and the part the skill most depends on |
| [Virtual Session Redesign](skills/fac-virtual-session-redesign/SKILL.md) | An in-room plan rebuilt for a video call, an interaction per block, breakout plan, producer role, plan B | The in-room day moves online as it is, and by the second hour people are answering email |

### 3 · Prepare the room

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Pre-Work and Invitation](skills/fac-pre-work-invitation/SKILL.md) | Invitation, one small "come with" ask, a pre-read from your own material, a reminder, the team's messages | People arrive not knowing why they are there, and nobody opens the pre-read |
| [Workshop Run Sheet](skills/fac-workshop-run-sheet/SKILL.md) | One table by clock time, pivot sentences, the move if it wobbles, kit list, two checklists | The session is Thursday, the agenda is agreed, and nothing is runnable yet |
| [Trainer and Facilitator Guide](skills/fac-trainer-facilitator-guide/SKILL.md) | Per segment its purpose, cue, key points, questions with good answers, activity setup, what to cut if late | A colleague delivers your session next month and all they have is your slides |
| [Training Slide Outline](skills/fac-training-slide-outline/SKILL.md) | Slide-by-slide outline with sentence headlines, one instruction slide per activity, cut list, accessibility notes | The deck is the session, and the trainer ends up reading it aloud |
| [Participant Handout](skills/fac-participant-handout/SKILL.md) | Guided notes, a worksheet per activity, the look-up content, the action plan page, an accessible layout | The handout is the slides printed six to a page |

### 4 · Run it

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Icebreakers and Session Openers](skills/fac-session-openers/SKILL.md) | Three openers tied to the first content block, a way to take part without personal disclosure, a virtual variant | You need an opener that does not make people cringe |
| [Session Agreements and Consent](skills/fac-session-agreements/SKILL.md) | Working agreements the room edits, the confidentiality rule, consent wording for any recording or AI note-taking | A recording tool joins the session by default and nobody was asked |
| [Difficult Moments Plan](skills/fac-difficult-moments-plan/SKILL.md) | A pre-thought move for each situation, the sentence to say, the block that shrinks | The room goes flat at 2pm and you are deciding what to do while forty people watch |
| [Activity Debrief Questions](skills/fac-activity-debrief-questions/SKILL.md) | A three-step question sequence per activity and for the close, follow-ups, a version for pairs | The exercise was lively and then the debrief is "so, how was that?" |
| [Wall and Board Capture](skills/fac-wall-board-capture/SKILL.md) | A capture file by block from wall photos and notes, counts as counts, unreadable items logged, questions flagged | The session just ended and everything it produced is on a wall that will be wiped tonight |

### 5 · Make it stick

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [If-Then Action Plan](skills/fac-if-then-action-plan/SKILL.md) | The page each participant fills, one "if this, then I will" plan, the first date, the obstacle, who they tell | The close is "one takeaway each" and by Monday nobody remembers theirs |
| [Practice Assignment](skills/fac-practice-assignment/SKILL.md) | One real task at work, what good looks like, a peer partner, how it comes back | People practised in the room and the first real attempt is left to chance |
| [Post-Session Follow-Up Sequence](skills/fac-follow-up-sequence/SKILL.md) | The messages after the session, one job each, dates you set, a way to opt out | The session went well, and then nothing happened |
| [Spaced Retrieval Plan](skills/fac-spaced-retrieval-plan/SKILL.md) | The few things people must recall, a question or scenario for each, gaps that widen, results by question | People could do it in the room and freeze a month later |
| [Manager Briefing Note](skills/fac-manager-briefing-note/SKILL.md) | One short message to managers before and one after, the one question to ask, what they will not receive | The manager approved the day off and has no idea what it was for |
| [Manager Check-In Guide](skills/fac-manager-check-in-guide/SKILL.md) | Before and after conversations in a manager's words, check-ins in existing meetings, a barrier log of conditions | Learners go back to a manager who never asks what they learned |

### 6 · Review and improve

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Session Feedback Form](skills/fac-session-feedback-form/SKILL.md) | Four to six questions with concrete answer options, two open prompts, an aggregate summary | The form says everyone loved it and tells you nothing to change |
| [Session Summary and Decisions](skills/fac-session-summary/SKILL.md) | The record against the brief, decisions and owners, themes with their items, vote counts, parking lot | The session was yesterday and you need to send something today |
| [Facilitator Retro](skills/fac-facilitator-retro/SKILL.md) | Six questions in fifteen minutes, a half-page note, timing corrections per activity | You run the same session next month and cannot remember what you swore to change |
| [Sponsor Debrief](skills/fac-sponsor-debrief/SKILL.md) | A one-page debrief for the sponsor call, aggregate themes, what the sponsor and managers do now, the decision asked | The sponsor asks "so, how did it go?" and all you have is "they seemed engaged" |

### 7 · Beyond one session

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Training Proposal](skills/fac-training-proposal/SKILL.md) | The situation in the client's words, the outcome, the design in stages, what the client provides, options with the price left blank | A client asks for "a quote for a one-day workshop" on something that took years to build |
| [70-20-10 Programme Plan](skills/fac-70-20-10-programme-plan/SKILL.md) | Real assignments at work, coaching and peer pairs tied to them, live sessions placed before the work that uses them | The client wants the two-day course again, and you know two days will not do it |
| [Train-the-Trainer Handover](skills/fac-train-the-trainer-handover/SKILL.md) | Delivery learning points, a modelled segment, teach-back slots, practice rounds, delivery notes, a readiness conversation | The programme has to run in five places and the plan is one walkthrough of the deck |

Need the wall saved before it is wiped?          -> Wall and Board Capture

## Example prompts

- "Run fac-session-purpose-brief. The client wants a half-day workshop on feedback for new managers. Here is their email. Tell me honestly whether this should be a session at all."
- "The sponsor sent me a very long deck for a 90-minute slot. Run fac-session-time-budget and show me what fits, what moves to the handout, and a cut list I can defend."
- "Run fac-difficult-moments-plan for this agenda. I am on my own with a large group and one block lands right after lunch."
- "Here are six photos of the walls from today. Run fac-wall-board-capture and keep the room's own words. Flag anything you cannot read while I can still check."
- "The session was last week and went well. Run fac-follow-up-sequence from this plan and write messages short enough to read on a phone."
- "Run fac-manager-briefing-note. Participants' managers approved the day and know nothing else. I need one message before and one after."

## Quality bar

- **One method, applied properly.** Each skill uses the real mechanics of its method (the order of the questions, the blocks, the rounds, the columns), not a generic "gather, analyse, recommend".
- **It tells you where it breaks.** Every skill says when not to use it and which skill here fits instead.
- **Same shape every time.** A fixed output template, a short "done when" check, and a decision a named person makes.
- **No invented facts.** No made-up numbers, benchmarks, quotes or results, and no forgetting or retention statistic anywhere. Every duration, gap and threshold is yours to set; missing data becomes a bracketed placeholder.
- **A person checks what participants will see.** Activities, slides, handouts and role plays carry a check a trainer runs before they reach the room.
- **Sessions are reviewed, people are not.** Feedback rates the session, never the trainer or a participant. Results are read in aggregate, small groups are suppressed, and nobody is sorted into a type.
- **The red line:** Claude prepares the session and the follow-up; you read the room and decide what happens in it, no participant is ever graded, ranked or profiled, and nothing is recorded without their consent.
