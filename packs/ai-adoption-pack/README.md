# Claude Guide for AI Adoption: 32 Claude Skills

32 Claude skills for getting a team to use AI well, from the first pilot to the habit. For L&D and enablement people, change leads and independent consultants who run AI adoption work.

**Guide and download:** [90n.ai/resources/ai-adoption-pack](https://90n.ai/resources/ai-adoption-pack)

## What this is

The guide follows AI adoption in the order the work happens. It finds where AI helps (the brief, an anonymous starting point survey, a use case map role by role, a barrier diagnosis), starts small (a pilot with a stop rule, real involvement, a capacity check), teaches it (a basics curriculum built on judgement, hands-on sessions, practice tasks from real work, an output check, a job aid, a train-the-trainer kit), supports managers (a manager kit, a team agreement, a follow-up guide, an agreement about freed time), makes it safe to question (the rules in plain language, an honest answer on jobs, routes for concerns and a log of what happened to them), measures what changed (measures agreed before the pilot, an evidence review, use stories, an executive update) and sustains it (a champions network, a shared prompt library, a review of local adaptations, the rollout decision and a quarterly habit review). The last skill loops back to the first. Each skill keeps one method inside, with the inputs it needs, where it breaks, a fixed output template, a finish line and the next skill to run. Where a skill fits a Claude surface (Claude Docs, Claude Slides, Projects), it says so, and beta features are marked beta.

Every skill holds one line: Adoption is something people choose: Claude prepares the plan, the sessions and the evidence, a named person makes every call, nobody's use of AI is tracked, scored or ranked, and no saving is claimed that the organisation's own evidence does not show.

```
Find where it helps -> Start small -> Teach it -> Support managers -> Make it safe to question -> Measure what changed -> Sustain it
```

## Install

### In Claude Code

```
/plugin marketplace add polar-bear-org/claude-skills
/plugin install ai-adoption-pack@90n-skills
```

### In Claude.ai

1. Turn on "Code execution and file creation" under Settings > Capabilities.
2. Go to Customize > Skills, click "+", choose "+ Create skill", then "Upload a skill", and upload the skill's zip from the `install/` folder. Repeat for each skill you want.
3. On Team and Enterprise plans, an owner first enables "Cloud code execution and file creation" and "Skills" under Organization settings > Plugins & skills (Policy tab), and can upload skills for the whole organization.

## The skills

### 1 · Find where it helps

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [AI Adoption Brief](skills/adopt-ai-adoption-brief/SKILL.md) | One observable work change per role, what stops as well as starts, scope, sponsor and decision owner, the first check with the people affected | The instruction is "get everyone using AI" and nobody has said what anyone should do differently on Monday |
| [AI Starting Point Survey](skills/adopt-ai-starting-point-survey/SKILL.md) | 10 to 15 anonymous questions, a minimum group size you set, a never-asked list, a team-level summary template | You are about to plan training for people you have never asked |
| [AI Use Case Map](skills/adopt-ai-use-case-map/SKILL.md) | Recurring tasks role by role, where AI helps and where it does not, what is off limits, who checks, the first three use cases to try | People say "I do not see what I would use it for" |
| [AI Adoption Barrier Diagnosis](skills/adopt-barrier-diagnosis/SKILL.md) | Competing explanations with evidence for and against, the smallest respectful check, a support matched to each barrier | People are not using it and the first proposal is another training |

### 2 · Start small

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [AI Pilot Plan](skills/adopt-ai-pilot-plan/SKILL.md) | The one question the pilot answers, a prediction written first, measures with definitions, stop and rollback conditions, the review rule | The choice is between everyone at once and a pilot that proves nothing |
| [Team Involvement Plan](skills/adopt-team-involvement-plan/SKILL.md) | Decided, open and unknown, who is affected, three concrete questions, channels with a private route, a ledger of what was heard | The rollout is "consulted" after the decision and people can tell |
| [Adoption Capacity Check](skills/adopt-capacity-check/SKILL.md) | Learning and practice time per role, current load, the gap, what can pause or move, options with trade-offs | People are told to learn AI on top of a full week |

### 3 · Teach it

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [AI Basics Curriculum](skills/adopt-ai-basics-curriculum/SKILL.md) | A short path by role built on four judgements, a practice in each unit, what is left out and why, a refresh date | The AI training on offer is a tool tour and a prompt list that dates in a month |
| [Hands-On AI Session Plan](skills/adopt-hands-on-session-plan/SKILL.md) | A timed plan where each person brings one real task, a demonstration, a practice round, a compare-and-check round, a no-screens fallback | The last AI session was a webinar where nobody touched the tool |
| [AI Practice Tasks](skills/adopt-ai-practice-tasks/SKILL.md) | Three to five tasks from the team's own work, a first attempt before any model answer, a common exception, a check of the work | People left the session keen and never opened the tool again |
| [AI Output Check](skills/adopt-ai-output-check/SKILL.md) | A one-page check a person runs before using AI output for one type of work, with a stop condition and who signs | People were taught to prompt and nobody was taught to check |
| [AI Job Aid](skills/adopt-ai-job-aid/SKILL.md) | One card for one task: when to use AI, what to give it, what to check, what never goes in, who to ask | People need the answer in the middle of the task, not a module |
| [AI Train-the-Trainer Kit](skills/adopt-ai-train-the-trainer/SKILL.md) | Delivery learning points, a teach-back plan, practice rounds that rise in difficulty, a readiness conversation with no score | One person cannot teach everyone and the internal trainers are a few weeks ahead of the room |

### 4 · Support managers

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Manager AI Kit](skills/adopt-manager-ai-kit/SKILL.md) | What the manager says, shows, asks and removes, and what a manager never does | The manager never mentions AI, or mentions it only as a target |
| [Team AI Agreement](skills/adopt-team-ai-agreement/SKILL.md) | Agreed uses per task, a never-put-in list, what checking means, whose name is on the work, a review date | Some people use AI for everything, some refuse, and nobody has said what is fine |
| [Manager Follow-Up Guide](skills/adopt-manager-follow-up/SKILL.md) | For one session: the conversation before, the first real task after, the conversation after, check-ins, a barrier log read in aggregate | People come back from the AI session to a full diary and a manager who never asks |
| [Freed Time Agreement](skills/adopt-freed-time-agreement/SKILL.md) | A team conversation guide and a one-page record of what takes less effort, what stops, where freed time goes, a workload check date | The time AI saves quietly turns into more tasks and longer days |

### 5 · Make it safe to question

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [AI Rules Explainer](skills/adopt-ai-rules-explainer/SKILL.md) | Your organisation's own AI rules as can, cannot and ask first, with real task examples and who to ask | The policy is eleven pages long and people still do not know what they can paste |
| [AI and Jobs Answer](skills/adopt-ai-jobs-answer/SKILL.md) | The questions people are asking, and for each what is decided, open and unknown, and who gives the answer | "Will this replace my job?" is in the room at every session |
| [AI Concern Statement](skills/adopt-ai-concern-statement/SKILL.md) | One person's concern in their own words: what I saw, what I think it means, what could follow, what I am asking for | Someone can see a problem and cannot say it without sounding against progress |
| [Speak-Up Practice Round](skills/adopt-speak-up-practice/SKILL.md) | A short rehearsal one turn at a time, comments on the choices made, a revised opening, the next step they choose | The concern is written and the conversation with the sponsor is tomorrow |
| [Team Challenge Invitation](skills/adopt-team-challenge-invitation/SKILL.md) | An invitation to question the plan before it is final, questions sent ahead, spoken, written and private routes, a response commitment | The only people who speak are the ones who already agree |
| [Concern Response Log](skills/adopt-concern-response-log/SKILL.md) | For each concern: the substance checked, what changed, what did not and why, owners and dates, a shareable update | Concerns were collected and nobody ever heard what happened to them |

### 6 · Measure what changed

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [AI Value Measures](skills/adopt-ai-value-measures/SKILL.md) | Four to six measures agreed before the pilot, each with a definition, a source and a baseline, one balancing measure for workload | The only measure anyone has proposed is "hours saved", self-reported |
| [AI Adoption Evidence Review](skills/adopt-adoption-evidence/SKILL.md) | Use, usefulness and side effects kept apart at team level, counts beside percentages, what the evidence cannot show | The dashboard says most people logged in and the sponsor reads that as adoption |
| [AI Use Stories](skills/adopt-ai-use-stories/SKILL.md) | A consent note, an interview guide, three to five anonymous accounts of real use, each marked as one case | The numbers are thin and the examples in circulation are demos |
| [AI Adoption Executive Update](skills/adopt-executive-update/SKILL.md) | One page, bottom line first: what changed, the evidence, what it does not show, concerns raised, the decision asked | Executives want one number for AI and the honest answer is more useful |

### 7 · Sustain it

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [AI Champions Network](skills/adopt-champions-network/SKILL.md) | A charter for a volunteer peer network: the role, protected time, how people join and leave, the route to the sponsor | Three enthusiasts answer everyone's questions in their own time and are about to stop |
| [Shared Prompt Library](skills/adopt-shared-prompt-library/SKILL.md) | The entry format, how entries are proposed, tested, updated and retired, where the library lives, the review rhythm | Good prompts live in private chats and the shared folder holds forty untested ones |
| [Local Adaptation Review](skills/adopt-local-adaptation-review/SKILL.md) | One team's variation: what changed and why, the safeguard it must keep, a bounded test with a rollback, a versioned note | Teams are quietly changing the agreed way of working with AI |
| [AI Rollout Decision](skills/adopt-ai-rollout-decision/SKILL.md) | The pilot's evidence and its limits, how the next teams differ, options with trade-offs, pause rules, dissent on record | The pilot went well with volunteers and the sponsor wants everyone on it next month |
| [AI Habit Review](skills/adopt-ai-habit-review/SKILL.md) | A quarterly review: the original need, current evidence, what changed in the tool, what to keep, simplify, refresh or retire | The launch was six months ago and half the team is back to the old way |

## Example prompts

- "Run adopt-ai-adoption-brief. The instruction from leadership is 'get everyone using AI by the end of the quarter'. Here are the three roles in scope and what they do each week."
- "People are not using the tool and I have been asked for another training. Run adopt-barrier-diagnosis with these notes from two team leads before I book anything."
- "Run adopt-hands-on-session-plan for a 90-minute session where every person brings one real task. Include what I do if the tool gives a wrong answer live."
- "Here is our AI policy. Run adopt-ai-rules-explainer and give me can, cannot and ask first, with examples from a support team's real tasks. Mark every legal point for our adviser."
- "The sponsor wants hours saved. Run adopt-ai-value-measures and help me agree four to six measures we already have data for, before the pilot starts."
- "The pilot went well with volunteers. Run adopt-ai-rollout-decision with these results and show how the next teams differ from the pilot teams."

## Quality bar

- **One method, applied properly.** Each skill uses the real mechanics of its method (the questions, the levels, the order of the pass), not a generic "gather, analyse, recommend".
- **It tells you where it breaks.** Every skill says when not to use it and which skill in the guide fits instead.
- **Same shape every time.** A fixed output template, a short "done when" check, and a decision that a named person makes.
- **No invented facts.** No made-up adoption rates, savings, quotes or findings. Missing data becomes a bracketed placeholder or an open question, and no productivity result is promised.
- **Team level only.** Nothing builds a list, score or ranking of who uses AI. Surveys are anonymous, results are reported by team, small groups are merged or suppressed, champions are volunteers and stories are told with consent.
- **Rules and law stay with their owners.** The skills explain rules your organisation already has. Every legal, data or employment point ends with "check with a qualified adviser".
- **The red line:** Adoption is something people choose: Claude prepares the plan, the sessions and the evidence, a named person makes every call, nobody's use of AI is tracked, scored or ranked, and no saving is claimed that the organisation's own evidence does not show.
