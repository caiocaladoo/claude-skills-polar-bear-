# Claude for Fractional Leaders: 31 Claude Skills

31 Claude skills for running a fractional engagement from the first retainer to the renewal, and several of them at once. For fractional and interim executives, and senior independents moving to that model.

**Guide and download:** [meet-polar-bear.com/skills/fractional-leaders-pack](https://meet-polar-bear.com/skills/fractional-leaders-pack)

## What this is

The skills follow one engagement in order, then the week across all of them. You position the role and answer "why not a full-time hire, or our own AI?". You shape and price the seat, sign a statement of work and agree a working charter. You spend the first 30 days on a diagnostic that ends on a date and a 90-day plan with baselines. You plan your capacity and your week across clients and keep one log per client. You report results, not activity, to the CEO and the board, and you warn early. You settle how the team works on your off days, frame and record decisions, and plan for a sponsor change. Then you review the renewal, price scope changes, redesign or raise the retainer, and hand over. Each skill produces one artifact and hands over to the next.

The red line: Claude prepares the diagnostic, the plan and the update from what you give it; it never invents a finding, a number or a client quote, never moves one client's information into another client's work, and never speaks to the client or the board for you.

```
Position the role -> Shape and price the engagement -> The first 30 days -> Run the week across clients -> Report -> Stakeholders and decisions -> Renew, expand, hand over
```

### In Claude.ai

- Turn on "Code execution and file creation" under Settings > Capabilities.
- Then go to Customize > Skills, click "+", choose "+ Create skill", then "Upload a skill", and upload the skill's zip from the `install/` folder.
- On Team and Enterprise plans, an owner enables "Cloud code execution and file creation" and "Skills" under Organization settings > Plugins & skills (Policy tab), and can upload skills for the whole organization.

Keep each client apart: one Claude Project per client where you have Projects (the redesigned Projects are in beta on select plans), or one separate chat and folder per client. Never run a skill for one client inside another client's workspace.

## The skills

### 1 · Position the role

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Fractional Positioning Statement](skills/frac-positioning-statement/SKILL.md) | What the company could do instead, your capabilities with proof, who cares most, one line a contact can repeat | People like you and still cannot say what a fractional leader in your function does |
| [Fractional or Full-Time Brief](skills/frac-fractional-or-full-time/SKILL.md) | One neutral page for the CEO with six options on the CEO's own criteria, what would change the choice, open facts | The CEO asks "why not just hire someone full time?" |
| [AI Objection Answer](skills/frac-ai-objection-answer/SKILL.md) | Their exact words, what is true in them, what the seat gives with proof, an offer to show how you work, your reply | The CEO says "now that AI does half of this, do we still need two days a week?" |

### 2 · Shape and price the engagement

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Engagement Discovery Questions](skills/frac-discovery-questions/SKILL.md) | One-page question guide for the scoping call, a "listen for" column, a blank answer grid, gaps to follow up | The scoping call ends with "send me a proposal" and you still do not know the outcome or who decides |
| [Good-Better-Best Engagement Options](skills/frac-engagement-options/SKILL.md) | Three real shapes of the seat by outcome, days and decision rights, what each leaves out, a decoy check | You sent one monthly figure and got a yes or a no, mostly a no |
| [Fractional Pricing Models](skills/frac-pricing-models/SKILL.md) | Six fee models side by side, who carries the overrun, what AI speed does to each, the overflow rule, your pick | You price by the hour and every faster week earns you less |
| [Retainer Conversation Prep](skills/frac-price-conversation-prep/SKILL.md) | Your number, your floor, your real alternative, the CEO's interests, your first two sentences, your trades | The fee call is tomorrow and you know you will offer money off before anyone asks |
| [Fractional Statement of Work](skills/frac-statement-of-work/SKILL.md) | The signed baseline with outcome, days, deliverables and acceptance, exclusions, overflow rule, exit triggers, adviser list | The fee is agreed and you can already hear "can you also" |

### 3 · The first 30 days

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Engagement Charter](skills/frac-engagement-charter/SKILL.md) | One working page with the outcome, when your days fall, three decision-rights lists, response times, review dates | You were hired to lead and every decision still goes back to the founder |
| [Fractional Onboarding Checklist](skills/frac-onboarding-checklist/SKILL.md) | The request list before day one with documents, access, introductions, the announcement, rhythm, first deliverable | Week two and you are still waiting for logins and nobody told the team who you are |
| [Stakeholder Map](skills/frac-stakeholder-map/SKILL.md) | People by role in the work, what each has said they need, two-way dependencies, unknowns turned into questions | You know the CEO well and nobody else, and the work depends on four other people |
| [First 30 Days Diagnostic](skills/frac-first-30-days-diagnostic/SKILL.md) | Diagnostic questions, interview guide, evidence log, a written assessment, ranked initiatives, the day-30 readout | Day 30 is near and you have a notebook of impressions instead of an assessment |
| [90-Day Plan](skills/frac-90-day-plan/SKILL.md) | Three objectives at most with measure, baseline and targets the CEO sets, a phased workplan, the day-90 decision | Month three is still discovery and nobody can say what was promised |

### 4 · Run the week across clients

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Client Capacity Plan](skills/frac-capacity-plan/SKILL.md) | Contracted days against focused days, time counted once, the gap, the options, a monthly ledger | Four clients fit the calendar and still do not fit the week |
| [Week Plan Across Clients](skills/frac-week-plan/SKILL.md) | A week template by client day or half-day, focus blocks, one triage slot, the version clients see | You switch between three companies before lunch and finish none of the thinking |
| [Client Log](skills/frac-client-log/SKILL.md) | One running log per client with decisions, commitments, open asks, days used, and a "where we left off" page | Every Tuesday starts with twenty minutes of remembering what you promised whom |
| [Weekly Review Across Clients](skills/frac-weekly-review/SKILL.md) | Expected against actual per client, days used against agreed, one thing to drop or renegotiate, next week's list | Friday arrives and you cannot say which client got more than they pay for |

### 5 · Report

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Weekly CEO Update](skills/frac-weekly-ceo-update/SKILL.md) | Bottom line first, results against the plan, status as it is, decisions needed with a date, scope asks | Your update is a list of meetings and the CEO still asks what you did |
| [Monthly Value Report](skills/frac-monthly-value-report/SKILL.md) | Agreed against delivered, the short scoreboard with sources, days used, scope changes, what stays open | The renewal is two months away and you have no page that shows what the seat changed |
| [Board Update](skills/frac-board-update/SKILL.md) | One page in six fixed blocks, stable definitions in footnotes, an appendix, the asks owned by the CEO | The CEO asks for "a few slides for the board" by Thursday |
| [Early Warning Note](skills/frac-early-warning-note/SKILL.md) | What changed, the evidence, known against unknown, the implication, options with their cost, the ask and its date | A target will be missed and you are tempted to wait for the monthly report |

### 6 · Stakeholders and decisions

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Team Interface Agreement](skills/frac-team-interface-agreement/SKILL.md) | One page with the complete request, who decides without you, response window, urgent route, a one-cycle trial | Work stops on the days you are with another client |
| [Relationship Repair Plan](skills/frac-relationship-repair-plan/SKILL.md) | Facts against accounts, a short acknowledgment, remedies in your authority, one prevention change, a follow-up | A commitment between you and a peer was missed and the work has gone cold |
| [Decision Brief](skills/frac-decision-brief/SKILL.md) | The question around an outcome, the owner, three to five real options with the case for and against, unknowns | The leadership team has discussed the same question three times and nobody has framed it |
| [Decision Record](skills/frac-decision-record/SKILL.md) | Status, owner, the choice and reasons, options rejected, dissent, actions, the review date and reopening rule | A decision made on your day gets reopened on a day you are not there |
| [Sponsor Change Plan](skills/frac-sponsor-change-plan/SKILL.md) | The introduction to ask for, a handover call agenda, the new sponsor's stated goals, relationships to build, a revised plan | The person who hired you is leaving, or only one person there knows why you matter |

### 7 · Renew, expand, hand over

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Renewal Review](skills/frac-renewal-review/SKILL.md) | Results against the charter, days used, strategic against reactive time, five options, the decision and its date | The contract is about to roll over, or run out, and nobody has looked at results |
| [Scope Change Request](skills/frac-scope-change-request/SKILL.md) | The ask in one line, the baseline line it touches, impact in days and fee, four options, a change log entry | "Could you also sit in on hiring?" arrives for the third time this month |
| [Retainer Redesign](skills/frac-retainer-redesign/SKILL.md) | The retainer as it stands, what the client values, five shapes, the move you choose, a conversation outline | The role has outgrown, or shrunk below, the days you sold |
| [Retainer Increase Plan](skills/frac-price-increase-plan/SKILL.md) | Per client the fee against your floor, the new fee you set, timing and notice, the order of conversations | You have put off raising the fee at three renewals in a row |
| [Handover Note](skills/frac-handover-note/SKILL.md) | What was built and where it lives, the decision log, open work, relationships handed over, access to remove, a timeline | The engagement ends in a month and everything is still in your head |

Need an update the CEO reads?  -> Weekly CEO Update
Need a page that shows what the seat changed?  -> Monthly Value Report
Need one page for the board?  -> Board Update

## Example prompts

- "Run frac-engagement-charter. Here is the signed statement of work and my notes from the kickoff with the CEO. Draft the three decision-rights lists for us to go through on Monday."
- "Run frac-first-30-days-diagnostic. Day 30 is in nine days. Here are my interview notes and the documents I was given. Mark every finding observed, reported or inferred."
- "Run frac-weekly-ceo-update from this week's client log. Lead with the result against objective 2 and list the two scope asks that go beyond my agreed days."
- "Run frac-capacity-plan. I have three clients and a fourth asking for two days a week. Here are the contracted days and my fixed meetings."
- "Run frac-scope-change-request. The CEO asked me to sit in on hiring interviews. Here is the statement of work line on exclusions and my day rate."
- "Run frac-ai-objection-answer. The CEO said this in our one-to-one, word for word. I want a spoken reply and a short written one."

## Quality bar

- Every finding, number and quote comes from what you paste in. Blanks stay blank, and unknowns are written "unknown" and turned into questions.
- One client per run and per workspace. Nothing from one client's files, figures or wording is used in another client's work.
- Nothing grades, ranks or scores a person. Maps record roles and what each person has said they need.
- No rate, retainer level or benchmark is ever suggested. Every fee and target is yours or the CEO's.
- Contract, status, tax and notice points are listed as questions: check with a qualified adviser.
- Your own AI use is stated, never hidden, and the client's own AI is treated as a fair option.
- The red line: Claude prepares the diagnostic, the plan and the update from what you give it; it never invents a finding, a number or a client quote, never moves one client's information into another client's work, and never speaks to the client or the board for you.
