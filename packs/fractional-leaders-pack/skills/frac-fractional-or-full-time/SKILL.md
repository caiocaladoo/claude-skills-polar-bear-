# Fractional or Full-Time Brief

## When To Use
The CEO asks "why not just hire someone full time?" and needs a fair page to show the board. They may like you, but they have to argue it in a room you are not in, against a director who wants a permanent hire and another who thinks the team can do it with AI. Use this when the honest question is what the choice in front of the company looks like.

## When Not To Use
If the question is any other decision the leadership team keeps circling, run Decision Brief. If you need your own standing statement of what you do, run Fractional Positioning Statement; this page does not argue for you.

## Inputs
- What the CEO said they are deciding, by when, and who approves it
- The criteria they named, in their units (cost in their figures, time to a result, risk, effort on their side, what happens after)
- What they said about a full-time hire, about their own AI plans, and about doing nothing, in their words
If you have none of this, I start from your call notes, mark every criterion as an assumption for the CEO to correct, and mark the output as a first draft.

## Approach
A decision frame with options and consequences, described generically: frame the decision first, then set out what each option leads to on criteria the decider owns, in plain words and with no made-up score. The judgment is neutrality. A page that quietly argues for the fractional seat is spotted in seconds and costs the CEO credibility with the board. A full-time hire is sometimes the right answer, and so is doing it in house with AI. The page says so where it is true.

## Workflow
1. Ask three questions, all at once: what decision does the CEO think they are making and by when; which criteria have they named; what have they said about hiring full time, or doing it in house with AI?
2. Frame the decision around an outcome, never around hiring you: "how [function] reaches [outcome] by [date]". Add who decides, who approves and what is already fixed.
3. Set out six columns: full-time hire, fractional seat, interim cover, adviser, in house with AI, do nothing. Write each so its strongest supporter would recognise it. Drop a column only if the CEO has ruled it out, and say so.
4. Fill consequences on the CEO's criteria only, in their units. Where a full-time hire or the AI route is stronger, write that first. An unknown stays "unknown: ask [role]". No weighted total.
5. Write "what would change the choice": for each option, the fact that would make it the right one (for example, the workload needing every day of the week makes the hire rational).
6. Read it as the most sceptical director. Any sentence that sells, any adjective standing in for evidence, any option drawn weak on purpose: rewrite it. Hand it to the CEO as their page to edit, in Claude Docs or as text.

## Output Format
```markdown
# Fractional or Full-Time Brief
Prepared for [CEO] · [date]
## The decision
[One sentence around the outcome] · Decide by [date] · Decides: [role] · Approves: [roles]
## Options compared
| Criterion (the CEO's) | Full-time hire | Fractional seat | Interim cover | Adviser | In house with AI | Do nothing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [cost, in their figures] | [consequence or unknown] | [..] | [..] | [..] | [..] | [..] |
| [time to a result] | [..] | [..] | [..] | [..] | [..] | [..] |
## What would change the choice
- [Option]: right if [fact]
## Open facts
- [Unknown]: ask [role]
## Decision
[CEO] chooses among these options by [date] and takes the page to [board or approver]; [CEO] owns and presents it.
```

## Done When
- The decision is framed around the company's outcome, not around hiring you
- A full-time hire, in house with AI and doing nothing each have a fair column
- Every consequence uses the CEO's criteria and figures, or reads "unknown"
- A sceptical reader could not tell which option you sell

## Quality Bar
- No option is drawn weak to make another look strong
- What the AI route and the full-time hire do well is stated before their limits
- Roles are named; no person on the client side is judged
- Employment and contractor points raised by any option: check with a qualified adviser
- No figure, salary, fee or result is invented, nothing comes from another client's engagement, and the CEO presents the page to the board, not you and not Claude
