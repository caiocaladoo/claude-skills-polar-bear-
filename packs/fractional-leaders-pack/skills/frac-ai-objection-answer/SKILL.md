# AI Objection Answer

## When To Use
The CEO says "now that AI does half of this, do we still need two days a week?" Your first reflex is to argue, or to offer a day back before anyone asked. This answers one question: what is true in what they said, and what does the company get from this seat that its own AI does not give it?

## When Not To Use
If the honest answer is that the shape of the seat should change, run Retainer Redesign. If the CEO is weighing the seat against every alternative for the board, run Fractional or Full-Time Brief. This skill prepares a reply; it never changes a fee.

## Inputs
- What the CEO said, word for word, and where; with the Gmail connector I can read the thread (read only; you send the reply)
- What this engagement has produced so far, from your client log, monthly reports or decision records for this client
- A plain note of where AI works in your own work for this client and where you check and decide
If you have none of this, I start from the CEO's words alone and mark the output as a first draft.

## Approach
Look for the interest behind the position, and separate the person from the problem, as the Harvard Program on Negotiation sets out on its public pages. "Do we still need two days" is a position. The interest under it is usually spend, doubt about value, speed, or a question from the board. The failure this prevents is the defensive reply that mocks their AI or hints at risks you cannot show; it loses the CEO's trust faster than the fee does.

## Workflow
1. Ask, all at once: what exactly did the CEO say, and where; who else will hear your answer (the board, a co-founder); will you answer on a call, in writing, or both?
2. Record their words as given. No paraphrase, no tidying, no guessing at tone.
3. Write what is true in it first, plainly: which parts of the work are now faster, for them and for you. If a task you were hired for now takes an hour, say so here.
4. Name the likely interest behind the position: spend, doubt about value, speed, pressure from the board. You pick the one that fits what you know, or you plan a question instead of a guess.
5. List what the seat gives that their AI does not: judgment on this company, accountability for a result, decisions made in the room, the check before something goes out. Each item needs proof from this engagement; without it the item is marked "claimed, not shown" and stays out of the reply.
6. Write the offer to show how you work: where AI does the work, where you check and decide. Openness is the strongest answer you have.
7. Draft the reply in your voice, spoken and written: the true part, the interest you heard, what they get, the offer. You edit it and say or send it yourself.

## Output Format
```markdown
# AI Objection Answer
Client: [name or code] · Date: [date] · Channel: [call / email / meeting]
## Their words
> [exactly as said or written]
## What is true in it
- [plain statement of what is now faster]
## The interest behind it
[spend / doubt about value / speed / pressure from the board] · Basis: [what you know] · Question if unsure: [question]
## What the seat gives that their AI does not
| What | Proof from this engagement | Shown or claimed |
|---|---|---|
| [judgment / accountability / decisions in the room / the check] | [decision, result, record] | [shown / claimed, not shown] |
## Offer to show how I work
[What you will show: where AI works, where you check and decide]
## Reply
Spoken: [three or four sentences] · Written: [short note in your voice]
## Decision
[You] decide by [date] whether to answer on a call or in writing, and you say or send it yourself.
```

## Done When
- The CEO's words are recorded exactly, never paraphrased
- The reply opens with what is true in the objection
- Every claim in the reply has proof marked "shown"
- The offer to show how you work is concrete

## Quality Bar
- No disparaging the client's AI, no fear claims, no hint of a risk you cannot show
- No line denies, blurs or plays down your own AI use
- Nothing judges the CEO's competence; their words are not mocked or passed on
- Proof comes from this client's engagement only, never from another client's results
- It answers honestly, never sells against AI, invents no result or quote, and you say it yourself
