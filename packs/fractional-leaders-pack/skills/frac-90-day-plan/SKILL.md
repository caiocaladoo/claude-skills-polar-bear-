# 90-Day Plan

## When To Use
Month three is still discovery and nobody can say what was promised. The diagnostic produced a long list, everyone agreed with all of it, and nothing has a baseline or an owner. This answers: which three things will be different in 90 days, how will we know, and who does the work on the days I am not there?

## When Not To Use
If the findings are not agreed yet, run First 30 Days Diagnostic first; a plan built on impressions inherits them. If you need the monthly readout of these same measures, run Monthly Value Report.

## Inputs
- The agreed findings and ranked initiatives from the diagnostic
- For each candidate objective: how it would be measured, where the figure comes from, and today's value if known
- Who on the client's team can own work, and your days
If you have none of this, I start from the outcome and your top three initiatives, leave every baseline and target blank and mark the plan as a first draft.

## Approach
Diagnose, decide, deliver: the 90-day shape described in a public playbook for fractional executives and an agency's article on the first 90 days, with each objective paired to a measure, a baseline, interim targets and a day-90 goal. The judgment is in the cutting: three objectives, a handful of measures, and a written list of what stops. The failure this prevents is the plan of fifteen priorities, in which a part-time leader has none, and the renewal conversation that turns into a debate about hours because nothing was baselined.

## Workflow
1. Ask at most three questions: which initiatives did the CEO agree at the readout; which measures already exist in their systems; who can own execution on their side?
2. Write three objectives at most, each as a result. For each: the measure, its definition in one line, the source system, and today's baseline. A baseline nobody has measured stays blank with a date to measure it; I never estimate one.
3. Leave the day 30, 60 and 90 targets for the CEO to set. I show the table and the arithmetic between baseline and target; I propose no target.
4. Build the workplan in three phases (foundations and early wins, stabilise, build and hand off), each line with an owner and a date. Mark which lines are yours and which belong to the team; a part-time leader who owns every line has no plan.
5. Write what stops or waits, so the plan fits the days. Then the risks, each with an owner and an early signal.
6. Set the reviews at day 30, 60 and 90, and state the decision due at day 90: continue as is, grow or shrink the seat, move to a full-time hire, or end.

## Output Format
```markdown
# 90-Day Plan
Client: [name] · Agreed with: [CEO] · Start: [date] · Reviews: day 30 [date], day 60 [date], day 90 [date]
## Objectives and measures
| Objective (a result) | Measure and definition | Source | Baseline today | Day 30 | Day 60 | Day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [objective] | [measure: definition] | [system] | [value, or blank until date] | [CEO sets] | [CEO sets] | [CEO sets] |
## Workplan
| Phase | Work | Owner | By |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundations and early wins | [work] | [you / team member] | [date] |
| Stabilise | [work] | [owner] | [date] |
| Build and hand off | [work] | [owner] | [date] |
## What stops or waits
- [work] stops or waits until [date], agreed by [role]
## Risks
| Risk | Owner | Early signal |
|---|---|---|
| [risk] | [owner] | [what you would see first] |
## Decision
[CEO] sets the targets and approves the plan by [date]; on day 90 [CEO] and [you] decide to continue, change the seat, hire full time or end.
```

## Done When
- There are three objectives or fewer, each with a measure, a definition and a source
- Every baseline is a measured value or a blank with a date
- Every target was set by the CEO
- Each workplan line has an owner, and something is listed under what stops

## Quality Bar
- Objectives are results, not activities such as "review" or "support"
- Definitions are fixed now; a later change shows both versions side by side
- Owners are named for work, never rated on it
- No baseline, target or benchmark is supplied by Claude
- Every figure comes from the client's own systems as you give it, none is invented, and no measure or target is carried over from another client's plan
