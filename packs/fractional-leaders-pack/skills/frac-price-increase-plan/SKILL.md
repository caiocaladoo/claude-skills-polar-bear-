# Retainer Increase Plan

## When To Use
You have put off raising the fee at three renewals in a row. The role grew, your costs rose, and the old number stays because the conversation feels risky with a client you see every week. Use this to decide, per client, whether the fee moves, to what, from when, and what you will do if the answer is no.

## When Not To Use
If the problem is what the retainer buys and not its level, run Retainer Redesign. If you are setting the fee of a new engagement, run Retainer Conversation Prep.

## Inputs
- Per client, by name or code: the current fee, your own floor, the contract terms on fee changes and notice
- Value delivered with evidence you can point to (monthly reports, results, what the client said in writing)
- Your capacity plan: what you would do with each client's days if they left
If you have none of this, I start from your current fees and your floor, and mark the plan as a first draft.

## Approach
A price review against your own floor and the value you delivered, a practitioner convention: the reason for a new fee is your cost and your work, never what others are said to charge. The judgment is per client: a client above your floor with strong evidence of value is a different conversation from one below it with thin evidence. For a fractional seat the change is a conversation with one sponsor, so the plan ends in talking points, not a letter. The failure this prevents is the flat increase announced to everyone in the same week.

## Workflow
1. Ask at most three questions: which clients are in scope; from what date do you want new fees; is any client in the middle of a renewal or a sponsor change?
2. Lay out each client: fee, floor, value delivered with its evidence. Flag any fee below your floor first.
3. You write the new fee on each line. I show the arithmetic (gap to floor, difference per month) and suggest no rate, no percentage and no market level.
4. Take timing and notice from each contract. Any question about notice or the right to change the fee: check with a qualified adviser.
5. Say what changes for each client: the fee only, or the fee and the scope. Choose the order of conversations and write why. For each client, write what you would do with the days if they left, so the plan does not rest on fear.
6. Draft the points for each conversation in your words: the new fee and its date first, what they keep getting, one true line on how you work now, AI included, and room for them to respond. No apology, no pressure, no deadline tricks, no comparison with the market or with another client.

## Output Format
```markdown
# Retainer Increase Plan
Effective from: [date] · Internal page, not shared with any client
## Clients today
| Client (name or code) | Current fee | My floor | Value delivered (evidence) | Contract: notice and fee change terms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [client] | [your figure] | [your figure] | [what, and where it is written] | [terms, or to check] |
## New fees
| Client | New fee (you set) | What changes for them | Effective date | Notice given by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [client] | [your figure] | [fee only / fee and scope] | [date] | [date] |
## Order of conversations
| Order | Client | Why this order | Date | If they leave: what I do with the days |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [1] | [client] | [one line] | [date] | [other work, pipeline, rest] |
## Points for each conversation
[Client]: [the new fee and date] · [what they keep] · [one true line on how I work now] · [question that leaves room]
## Questions for an adviser
- [notice or contract question] (check with a qualified adviser)
## Decision
[You] confirm each new fee and conversation date by [date], and have each conversation yourself.
```

## Done When
- Every new fee is written by you and sits at or above your floor, or the exception is explained
- Each client has an effective date, a notice date and a place in the order
- Each client has a line for what you do with the days if they leave
- No point for any conversation cites a market rate

## Quality Bar
- Clients are compared on fees, days and evidence, never on judgments about the people
- Value evidence is something you can show, not an impression
- Fee-only and fee-and-scope changes are kept apart
- The talking points state your AI use truthfully and carry no apology and no pressure
- The fees are yours alone; none is invented, no client's fee or terms are ever mentioned to another, and this page never leaves you
