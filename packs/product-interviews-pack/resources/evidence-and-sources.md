# Evidence and sources

Research checked 11 October 2026.

## What this pack claims

The pack claims that each skill applies a documented practice faithfully, attributes it to a public source, and states where it breaks. It claims that the rounds it prepares (product sense, metrics, prioritisation and execution, the portfolio or project walkthrough, behavioural and values rounds, the take-home) are rounds that employers describe in their own public interview guides, and that its position on AI in hiring matches a published candidate policy.

Nothing in the pack is legal advice: points on offers, contracts, pay, recording and personal data end with "check with a qualified adviser".

## Deliberate design choices

These are Polar Bear's own choices, not findings from the sources.

- **The red line.** Claude helps you prepare and practise from work you really did: it never invents a role, a result or a number, it stays out of the live interview, and when an employer asks what you used AI for, you tell them. S1 shows one employer asking the same of its candidates. Making it the rule of every skill, for every employer, is our position.
- **Evidence rows.** The row with a "how you know" column, the claim map and the do-not-claim list are our own practice methods. No source says they improve interview outcomes.
- **No scoring of people.** Employers use rating scales (S5). We do not reproduce one on a person. Practice notes describe the answer as present, thin or missing against a rubric the person can see, with no number and no overall verdict. Offers are scored against criteria the person wrote first, and the person decides.
- **Public professional facts only.** Notes on interviewers hold public professional history with a source and a date, are not kept in memory, and never guess at personality, private life or any protected characteristic. A thin profile stays thin.
- **Points, not finished messages.** Notes to former colleagues, referral asks and thank-yous are given as points the person writes and sends.
- **No pay figures.** No market figure or benchmark appears anywhere. Figures come from the employer's stated range or from the person, with a source and a date.
- **Adviser referrals.** Legal points on offers, contracts, pay, recording and personal data are listed for a qualified adviser and never stated as advice.
- **Roles only.** The pack names the reader's role and nothing else about them: no company size, sector or company type, in the skills or the examples. Employer interview guides appear in this file as links to the public page, as sources of a round format and never as places to apply. The one employer named in the skills is Anthropic, as the source of its own public candidate guidance and course.
- **Practice partner, not a prediction.** The case skills give a structure and a partner to practise with. They do not claim to match any employer's rubric, and no efficacy is claimed anywhere.
