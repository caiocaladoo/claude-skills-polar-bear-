# Claude Guide for Product Interviews: 32 Claude Skills

32 Claude skills for preparing product interviews from the job ad to the offer, built on work you really did. For product managers, product owners and product designers with years of shipped work.

**Guide and download:** [90n.ai/resources/product-interviews-pack](https://90n.ai/resources/product-interviews-pack)

## What this is

The pack follows one job search in order. You read the role first: what you want, what one ad really asks, the company, the people on the loop and what each round tests. You turn years of work into evidence rows, stories, a resume and a profile that say what you decided, not only what launched. You practise the rounds by their real names (product sense, metrics, prioritisation and execution, app critique), then the conversations (behavioural answers, the portfolio or project walkthrough, your own questions, a mock interview). You prepare an honest answer to "how do you use AI in your work" with a piece of work to show. You get in through people you already know, run each process without losing track, and compare offers against criteria you wrote before any offer existed. Each skill is one method or one artifact, with the inputs it needs, where it breaks, a fixed output template, a finish line and the next skill to run.

Every skill holds one line: Claude helps you prepare and practise from work you really did: it never invents a role, a result or a number, it stays out of the live interview, and when an employer asks what you used AI for, you tell them.

```
Read the role -> Tell your story -> Prepare the cases -> Prepare the conversations -> Show how you work with AI -> Get in through people -> Run the process -> Decide
```

## Install

### In Claude Code

```
/plugin marketplace add polar-bear-org/claude-skills
/plugin install product-interviews-pack@polar-bear-skills
```

### In Claude.ai

1. Turn on "Code execution and file creation" under Settings > Capabilities.
2. Go to Customize > Skills, click "+", choose "+ Create skill", then "Upload a skill", and upload the skill's zip from the `install/` folder. Repeat for each skill you want.
3. On Team and Enterprise plans, an owner first enables "Cloud code execution and file creation" and "Skills" under Organization settings > Plugins & skills (Policy tab), and can upload skills for the whole organization.

Every skill works in a normal chat with what you paste in. Some name an optional Claude surface: a Project to keep your inventory, stories and loop maps together, voice mode to practise answers aloud, web search in chat for company research, Claude Docs, Claude Slides and Claude Design for a document, a deck or a small prototype, and in the desktop app a Google Sheet created from the chat when Google Drive is connected. Practice happens before the interview. None of these skills runs during one.

## The skills

### 1 · Read the role

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Target Role Brief](skills/pmjob-target-role-brief/SKILL.md) | Two or three target roles with level, your criteria and deal-breakers, the words real ads use, each matched to your evidence | You are applying to everything and cannot say in one line what you want next |
| [Job Ad Breakdown](skills/pmjob-job-ad-breakdown/SKILL.md) | One ad read line by line: the problem behind the role, must-haves, level signals, the AI ask, implied rounds, your evidence, gaps | The ad lists twenty requirements and you cannot tell which three decide the hire |
| [Company Research Brief](skills/pmjob-company-research/SKILL.md) | What the product does and for whom, your own notes from using it, dated news with sources, three questions a website cannot answer | The interview is in three days and you could only describe the logo |
| [Interviewer Research Notes](skills/pmjob-interviewer-research/SKILL.md) | Per interviewer: public professional facts with source and date, likely angle by role, unknowns, one question only they could answer | You have five names on a calendar invite and know a job title for each |
| [Interview Loop Map](skills/pmjob-interview-loop-map/SKILL.md) | Every round with date, length, interviewer, format and what it assesses, the AI rule per stage, stories assigned, questions for the recruiter | Six rounds are booked and you are preparing the same answers for all of them |

### 2 · Tell your story

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Career Evidence Inventory](skills/pmjob-experience-inventory/SKILL.md) | One evidence row per piece of work, the unglamorous work logged on purpose, strengths backed by two rows, a do-not-claim list | Fifteen years of work and you cannot remember what you did in the role before last |
| [Interview Story Bank](skills/pmjob-story-bank/SKILL.md) | Eight to twelve stories in a two minute and a thirty second version, tagged by competency, with follow-ups and facts not to stretch | Every behavioural question gets the same launch story, told a little differently each time |
| [Tell Me About Yourself](skills/pmjob-tell-me-about-yourself/SKILL.md) | A ninety second answer in present, past, future order, a twenty second version, two true sentences for "why are you looking" | The first question of every interview is the one you answer worst |
| [Product Resume Bullets](skills/pmjob-resume-bullets/SKILL.md) | Two to four lines per role as challenge, action, result, with scope, your decision, your part apart from the team's, sourced numbers only | Your resume is a list of launches and reads like the job description |
| [LinkedIn Profile Rewrite](skills/pmjob-linkedin-profile/SKILL.md) | A section check, three headline options, an About in your own voice, a claim map, mismatches with the resume to fix first | Your profile undersells senior work, or a chatbot rewrote it and it reads like everyone else's |

### 3 · Prepare the cases

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Product Sense Case](skills/pmjob-product-sense-case/SKILL.md) | A practice case on an open product or design question, then notes on your answer against a rubric you can see | You know the steps by heart and your answers still sound like a recited framework |
| [Metrics Case](skills/pmjob-metrics-case/SKILL.md) | Practice on choosing success metrics and on diagnosing a metric that moved, with your reasoning written out and checked | You say "I would look at the data" and cannot say which data or what would change your mind |
| [Prioritisation and Execution Case](skills/pmjob-execution-case/SKILL.md) | Practice on a trade-off question: options, what each costs and who pays, the call, the cut, the risk you accept | The question is "you have five things and room for two" and you answer by listing all five |
| [App Critique](skills/pmjob-app-critique/SKILL.md) | A practice critique of an app you choose: users, how it earns its place, flows walked through, the one change you would argue for | You practise by criticising random apps and it never turns into a method |

### 4 · Prepare the conversations

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Behavioural Interview Answers](skills/pmjob-behavioural-answers/SKILL.md) | For one round: the competencies tested, a story and a reserve story for each, the follow-ups and your honest answers | A values or leadership round is next and your stories all say "we" |
| [Portfolio and Project Walkthrough](skills/pmjob-work-walkthrough/SKILL.md) | A timed talk on two or three pieces of work: stakes, your role, three decisions, the mistake, the outcome, a slide outline, likely questions | You stopped designing every screen or writing every spec, and your walkthrough still shows screens and timelines |
| [Questions to Ask](skills/pmjob-questions-to-ask/SKILL.md) | Questions for each interviewer by role, website-answerable ones removed, three marked to ask first | "Do you have any questions for us" and you ask about the culture |
| [Mock Interview](skills/pmjob-mock-interview/SKILL.md) | A practice round with a stand-in interviewer and follow-ups, then notes on each answer as present, thin or missing | You have prepared on paper and never said any of it out loud |

### 5 · Show how you work with AI

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Candidate AI Use Plan](skills/pmjob-ai-use-plan/SKILL.md) | One page per employer: what they allow at each stage and where it is written, the question for the recruiter, the sentence you will say | You do not know whether using AI on the take-home is expected or forbidden, and you have not asked |
| [AI Fluency Answer](skills/pmjob-ai-fluency-answer/SKILL.md) | An answer to "how do you use AI in your work" from two or three real examples, in a short and a long version, with no tool list | The ad asks for AI fluency, your title has never had AI in it, and your answer is a list of tools |
| [AI Work Sample](skills/pmjob-ai-work-sample/SKILL.md) | A one page write-up of a real task, an optional small prototype labelled as a prototype, a confidentiality check | They will ask you to show it, not describe it |

### 6 · Get in through people

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Target Company List](skills/pmjob-target-company-list/SKILL.md) | Ten to twenty companies with a sourced reason each fits, the open role, who you know there, and a dropped list | Your list is five famous names and you have never checked who you know at any of them |
| [Reason to Write](skills/pmjob-reason-to-write/SKILL.md) | For each former colleague, one honest reason to get back in touch, what not to ask yet, points for a message you write | You have not spoken in six years and every draft turns into an ask by line two |
| [Informational Interview Questions](skills/pmjob-informational-questions/SKILL.md) | Questions for a conversation with an insider, website-answerable ones removed, five to ask first, a closing question | An insider agreed to twenty minutes and you are about to ask what the careers page already says |
| [Referral Ask](skills/pmjob-referral-ask/SKILL.md) | A readiness check on the relationship and the role, a plain "not yet" when it is, points for asking with an easy no | A role opened where a former colleague works and you are not sure you can ask |

### 7 · Run the process

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Recruiter Screen Prep](skills/pmjob-recruiter-screen/SKILL.md) | A check that an inbound message is real, then one page for the first call: why you are looking, timing, pay expectations, your questions | A recruiter wants thirty minutes tomorrow and will ask what salary you expect |
| [Take-Home Assignment Plan](skills/pmjob-take-home/SKILL.md) | The brief restated, questions to send first, a time box you set, the submission structure, an assumptions page, a note on AI use | The take-home says "spend as long as you need" and you are about to give it a week |
| [Interview Round Debrief](skills/pmjob-round-debrief/SKILL.md) | What was asked, what you answered, promises made, what to change in your stories, points for a specific thank-you | You walked out, wrote nothing down, and gave the same weak answer in the next loop |
| [Job Search Tracker](skills/pmjob-search-tracker/SKILL.md) | One row per process, a twenty minute weekly review, one nudge for a silent process and then a close | Five processes at five stages and you just missed a follow-up |

### 8 · Decide

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Offer Comparison](skills/pmjob-offer-comparison/SKILL.md) | Each offer, and staying where you are, against your own criteria and weights, with sources, unknowns and questions before you sign | Two offers, a deadline on one, and you are comparing base pay only |
| [Offer Negotiation Prep](skills/pmjob-negotiation-prep/SKILL.md) | Your real alternative, what you want and the least you would accept, the facts behind each ask, the order of the conversation | The offer is good and you know you should ask, and you have nothing but a feeling |
| [30-60-90 Day Plan](skills/pmjob-30-60-90-plan/SKILL.md) | A first draft for the new role: who to meet, what to learn before changing anything, one early result, what you will propose | You start in three weeks, or a final round asks what you would do in your first ninety days |

## Example prompts

- "Run pmjob-job-ad-breakdown on this ad. Tell me which three lines decide the hire and where my evidence is thin."
- "Run pmjob-experience-inventory. Interview me role by role, one question at a time, starting with my current job."
- "Run pmjob-interview-loop-map. Here is the recruiter's email with five rounds. What does each one assess, and which story goes where?"
- "Run pmjob-product-sense-case. Give me an open question, let me answer in full, then show me what was present, thin or missing."
- "Run pmjob-ai-fluency-answer. I have never had AI in my title. Here are three things I really did with it last quarter."
- "Run pmjob-offer-comparison with the criteria from my Target Role Brief. Two offers, plus staying where I am."

## Quality bar

- Every claim about you traces to an evidence row you can source. A missing number stays a placeholder.
- Your part is kept apart from the team's. Colleagues appear by role and nobody is judged.
- Nothing scores, ranks or grades you. Practice notes rate the answer against a rubric you can see, as present, thin or missing. Offers are scored against your own criteria, and you decide.
- Facts about companies and people come from public pages, with a source and a date. Unknown stays unknown. Nothing about an interviewer is kept in memory.
- Messages to people are given as points. You write and send them yourself.
- No pay figure comes from Claude. Figures come from the employer's stated range or from you, with a source and a date, and legal points on offers, contracts, recording and personal data end with "check with a qualified adviser".
- The red line: Claude helps you prepare and practise from work you really did: it never invents a role, a result or a number, it stays out of the live interview, and when an employer asks what you used AI for, you tell them.
