# Claude Guide for New Founders: 31 Claude Skills

31 Claude skills for the first months on your own, from your last job to your first clients. For new founders of one-person practices: consultants, coaches, advisers and trainers.

**Guide and download:** [meet-polar-bear.com/skills/new-founders-pack](https://meet-polar-bear.com/skills/new-founders-pack)

## What this is

You left a senior employed role and now you find your own clients. This guide follows the first months in order: decide what you sell, package and price it, tell people, start with the people who already know your work, reach out and talk, win the first client, then keep going once delivery starts. Each skill is one artifact or one named method, and each one hands over to the next. It ends at the first client: a short first proposal, the price conversation and a start note.

The red line: Claude prepares from your real career, your own contacts and your own numbers, never from a former employer's files; you choose who to talk to, put it in your own words and send it yourself, and nothing is invented, automated or sent for you.

```
Decide what you sell -> Package and price it -> Tell people -> Start with people who know you -> Reach out and talk -> Win the first client -> Keep going
```

## The skills

### 1 · Decide what you sell

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Career Proof Inventory](skills/start-career-proof-inventory/SKILL.md) | One row per piece of past work, who saw it, what you may say in public, your three strongest proofs | Your whole track record sits inside a former employer |
| [Ideal Client Profile](skills/start-ideal-client-profile/SKILL.md) | The situation and trigger, the job they hire you for, who decides, red flags for a first call | You would say yes to anyone who asked |
| [Point of View Statement](skills/start-point-of-view/SKILL.md) | Up to three beliefs in your words, the shift behind each, your evidence, where you might be wrong | Twenty years of opinions and none written under your own name |
| [Positioning Statement](skills/start-positioning-statement/SKILL.md) | Honest alternatives, capabilities with proof, one line a contact can repeat, a repeat-back test | Former colleagues like you and cannot say what you do now |

### 2 · Package and price it

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [First-Year Runway Plan](skills/start-runway-plan/SKILL.md) | Monthly costs from your records, months of runway, the income you need by when, three dated scenarios | You left a salary and do not know how many months you have |
| [Floor Rate Calculator](skills/start-floor-rate/SKILL.md) | Fully loaded monthly cost, real billable days, floor per day and per hour, what moves it most | Your only reference for a price is your old salary |
| [Productized Service Offer](skills/start-productized-offer/SKILL.md) | One named offer, fixed scope and timeline, exclusions, a price checked against your floor, a paid first step | Every conversation ends with "send me something" |

### 3 · Tell people

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Writing Voice Guide](skills/start-voice-guide/SKILL.md) | How you open, ask and sign off, words you use and never use, an AI-tell checklist, a before and after | Every draft sounds like everyone else's |
| [LinkedIn Headline](skills/start-linkedin-headline/SKILL.md) | Buyer words with their source, five options that differ on purpose, a read-aloud test, your pick | Your headline is your last job title, or "Founder" and nothing else |
| [LinkedIn About Section](skills/start-linkedin-about-section/SKILL.md) | The two opening lines, the buyer's situation, how you work, two proofs, one way to start a conversation | Your About was written to get you hired |
| [LinkedIn Experience Section](skills/start-linkedin-experience-section/SKILL.md) | A current entry for your practice, past roles as problems solved, a confidentiality check per line | Twenty years of job titles and no entry for the business you run |
| [Independence Announcement](skills/start-independence-announcement/SKILL.md) | Points for a public post in your words, a direct message version, what not to claim, a grammar check | You have told nobody you are on your own |
| [One-Page Offer](skills/start-one-page-offer/SKILL.md) | One page a contact can forward: who it is for, the problem, the offer, one proof, how to start | Someone says "send me something I can pass on" and all you have is a CV |

### 4 · Start with people who know you

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Former Employer Terms Check](skills/start-former-employer-terms/SKILL.md) | The clauses in your own contract, quoted with dates, what each may touch, questions for an adviser | You want to call former clients and colleagues and do not know what your contract allows |
| [Former Colleagues List](skills/start-former-colleagues-list/SKILL.md) | Who saw your work at each past employer, where they are now, a reason to write, a first run you pick | The list of people who know your work exists only in your head |
| [LinkedIn Data Export Guide](skills/start-linkedin-data-export/SKILL.md) | Request steps, files to use and leave out, column cleanup, where the copy lives, a deletion date | You want your whole network in one list without a tool that logs in as you |
| [Warm Network Map](skills/start-warm-network-map/SKILL.md) | A brief first, visible match with its reason, fit and closeness set by you, a run of about 10 | Years of connections and no idea who to talk to this week |

### 5 · Reach out and talk

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [Reconnect Message](skills/start-reconnect-message/SKILL.md) | A reason to write you choose, talking points with sources, a grammar-only check, a follow-up date | The first time they hear from you in years is the week you need work |
| [Introduction Ask](skills/start-introduction-ask/SKILL.md) | The moment check, one specific introduction, the easy no, what you offer in return | Asking former colleagues for help feels like begging |
| [Warm Introduction Request](skills/start-warm-intro-request/SKILL.md) | The path check, the ask to the connector, a forwardable blurb, double opt-in steps, the thank-you | Someone you know knows the person you need |
| [Coffee Chat Prep](skills/start-coffee-chat-prep/SKILL.md) | What you know with sources, three questions, one thing to offer, what you will not pitch | You fear turning a catch-up into a sales meeting |
| [Discovery Call Guide](skills/start-discovery-call-guide/SKILL.md) | An agenda to agree, questions written for this person, the cost of doing nothing, the next step | A contact says "we might need help with that" |
| [Discovery Call Recap](skills/start-discovery-call-recap/SKILL.md) | The problem in their words, how they decide, open questions, the next step, an email you send | A week later nobody remembers what was agreed |

### 6 · Win the first client

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [First Client Proposal](skills/start-first-client-proposal/SKILL.md) | One or two pages: their objective, phases, one to three real options, the fee, a dated next step | They asked for a proposal and you have never written one in your own name |
| [Price Conversation Prep](skills/start-price-conversation-prep/SKILL.md) | Your number and floor, your real alternative, their interests, your first two sentences, your trades | You already know you will offer a discount before they ask |
| [Former Employer Engagement Plan](skills/start-former-employer-engagement/SKILL.md) | Scope and end date, how requests reach you, the share of income you allow, questions for an adviser | Your first client is your old employer and it feels like your old job |
| [First Client Onboarding Note](skills/start-client-onboarding-note/SKILL.md) | A welcome note, the first two weeks, what you need from them, contact rhythm, invoicing, a first date | They said yes and there is no process behind you any more |

### 7 · Keep going

| Skill | What it does | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| [90-Day Practice Plan](skills/start-ninety-day-plan/SKILL.md) | One goal from your numbers, a warm-first channel mix, weekly actions you control, three checkpoints | You have never had to sell and nobody will give you a target |
| [Weekly Selling Hour](skills/start-weekly-selling-hour/SKILL.md) | Follow-ups due, warm signals, three people to reconnect with, one conversation to book, a log line | The first engagement started and you have written to nobody since |
| [Conversation Log](skills/start-conversation-log/SKILL.md) | Who and when, what they need, what was promised by whom and by when, the next date | You told three people "I will send you that" and forgot which three |
| [Monthly Practice Review](skills/start-monthly-practice-review/SKILL.md) | Income by client and source, your largest client's share, runway left, one thing to stop and one to change | One client pays every bill and you have not looked past that contract |

## Example prompts

- "Run start-career-proof-inventory. Here is my CV. Help me list what I did, what changed, and what I am allowed to say about it."
- "Here are my last three months of costs. How many months of runway do I have, and what is my floor per day?"
- "Here are my employment contract and my exit letter. List the clauses that touch who I can contact, with questions for my adviser."
- "Here is my Connections.csv and my two-line brief. Show me who visibly matches, and I will pick about 10 people for this month."
- "A former colleague just changed roles. Give me three points with sources, and I will write the message myself."
- "My old employer wants me back three days a week as a contractor. Help me set the scope, the end date and my limits."

## Quality bar

- Every proof, claim and example comes from your own career, in words you are allowed to use; nothing comes from a former employer's files.
- Every number is yours: costs, runway, floor, price and goals come from your records or stay as [placeholders]. No market rate, no benchmark, no income promise.
- Every fact about another person comes from a public work source, linked, or from your own records; what has no source is dropped or marked unknown.
- Nothing grades, ranks or scores people. Fit is to a brief and set by you; closeness describes how well you know someone, in your words.
- Warm before cold: the people who already know your work come first, in small runs you pick by hand. No sequences, no automation.
- Contract, worker status, tax and data protection points are question lists that end with "check with a qualified adviser".
- The red line: Claude prepares from your real career, your own contacts and your own numbers, never from a former employer's files; you choose who to talk to, put it in your own words and send it yourself, and nothing is invented, automated or sent for you.
